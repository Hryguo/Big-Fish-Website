Our Story

A Family Kitchen for the Neighborhood

Big Fish was born right here in San Jose. In our kitchen: three master chefs, each with over 30 years at the wok — with one goal: serve the best Chinese food in the neighborhood, at prices every family can afford.

Alongside the classics — General Tso's chicken, honey walnut prawns, Peking spare ribs, moo shu — we're proud to serve Korean-Chinese specialties (중화요리) like jajangmyeon, jjamppong and tangsuyuk, plus hand-made dumplings, xiao long bao and pan-fried pork buns.

Generous portions, honest prices, and a warm welcome. Come hungry!
